How a PO moves

From purchase order to GRN, on one system.

Crosmo Hub, our in-house order management system, runs every marketplace order and every B2B purchase order through the same five steps.

1
PO intakeVendor Hub and marketplace orders land in one queue; unmapped SKUs are held, never shipped blind.
2
ProductionStock check, printing and job work scheduled against the PO expiry date.
3
Pick, pack, labelSKU-wise picklists, carton labels and manifests generated by Crosmo Hub.
4
Scan and dispatchBarcode verification at the dock; e-invoice raised in Busy against the PO.
5
Appointment and GRNDelivery on the booked slot; GRN and payment tracked per PO until closed.
